## What this proves, and what it does not

It proves the host-side modules are individually correct against a transcription of their native
function, on the exact sources that were shipped.

It does not prove the chain composition. A module can be green and still be absent from the runtime —
that is precisely what EXP213-218 did, twice, with millions of calls per stage. Composition is a
separate check:

    scripts/check_witnesses.py --run <stage> --baseline <previous>     # with a baseline
    nm -u runtime/<stage>/build/*.o                                    # before linking

The CUDA modules (concat, get_rows, cpy, quantize, MoE weighted, mm_ids, FA, norm, rope, topk, ssm,
gdn, broadcast, activations, VMM pool, pinned pool, MMQ dispatch) are **not** covered by this table.
They were verified by byte-compare against the native kernel plus exact-token Apex runs, because
their correctness is a GPU-buffer equality, not a host reference.

## Reproduce

    python3 scripts/run_all_gates.py

The runner probes a few include-path variants per fixture, because the fixtures reference different
subsets of the native tree; two of them (EXP218) additionally link `unicode-data.cpp`.
