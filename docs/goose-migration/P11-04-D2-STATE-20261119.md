Checked 2026-11-19 on `main`, text level, no build.

All four are in the composed unit (`tools/build-main-runtime.sh`):

| module | in unit |
|---|---|

| `goose/cli/config-resolve.goose` | yes |

## Reached by a host, and where

| door | call site | which artefact |
|---|---|---|
| `cfg_validate` | `integration/goose-standalone/host_v4.c` and the S2.1 gate `integration/m1/gates/s21/fg_config_smoke.cpp` | the **standalone** binaries only; `host_v4.c` is adapter debt, not part of `libllama.so` |
| `fg_sp_*` (speculative doors) | `runtime/reconstructed/server-merge/server-context.cpp:100-102` resolves the synth door and calls it | the reconstructed runtime |
| `cc_*` (config resolve: env/flag names, defaults, `cc_atoi`, `cc_env_int`) | **none** | composed, unwired |

## What this means

- Configuration **validation** is wired where it is called (the standalone host), and it is not on
  the Apex inference path: nothing in `libllama.so` calls `cfg_validate`.
- Configuration **resolution** (`config-resolve.goose`, six doors) has no caller at all. It is the
  clearest example of the case rule 33 names: present in the unit, absent from every call site.
- Speculation is wired for the synthesis door on the reconstructed runtime. It is **not** D2's
  closing evidence on its own: the GBNF of the requirement - MTP, draft orchestration, accept/reject
  and rewind - is P6-11's subject and stays open there.

## What closing D2 needs

1. a real call site for the config-resolve doors, or a recorded decision that configuration is not
   resolved in this tree (the same accounting used for the absent CLI hosts in P10-02);
2. the removal of `host_v4.c` as adapter debt once the standalone host calls the unit directly,
   which is what the migration-status debt ledger already counts;
3. speculation: P6-11's acceptance run, not another wiring change.

Nothing here is claimed as measured.
