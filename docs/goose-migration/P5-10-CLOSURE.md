## Explicit limits

- The accepted profile runs one sequence (`-np 1`); multi-sequence is evidenced
  by the four-way concurrency run on the matched candidate, not by the fixed
  production profile.
- Shift is OFF_PATH (P5-09): the accepted profile never shifts.
- `state_read_data` keeps the native interleaved per-layer I/O at a coarser
  granularity than the ported per-span arithmetic (EXP366); this was already
  recorded under APEX.KV and is a buffer primitive, not a policy decision.

## Verdict

P5-10 closes on the milestone evidence above. The item is a closure of the
memory/KV subsystem on the real model, not a claim that every byte-level I/O
primitive is generated by Goose.
