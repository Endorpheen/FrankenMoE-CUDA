# EXP-2026-09-15-064: pinned ring depth 4 (follow-up to PRE-1)

Status: `REJECTED` (2026-09-15).
Kind: `performance`.

This card records a completed build-flag A/B that has already been run. The raw data
is under `results/archive/EXP-2026-09-15-064/`. The card itself starts no server,
loads no model, builds nothing and runs no curl. It is the written verdict for the
depth-4 follow-up to the accepted depth-3 candidate PRE-1 in
`experiments/HYPOTHESES-2026-09-15-runtime-speed.md`.

The candidate was built and passed its model-free gate in the same archive
(`results/archive/EXP-2026-09-15-064/REPORT.md`); this card is the model-run verdict
that follows it.

## Hypothesis

The accepted depth-3 ring (PRE-1, EXP-2026-09-15-062) hides part of the per-depth
slot wait behind the previous H2D copy. If the wait is still not fully hidden at
depth 3, a fourth 16 MiB pinned ring slot should hide more of it and shorten bulk
prefill latency further. This follow-up tests depth 4 against the depth-3 candidate
itself, which is now the accepted control.

Accept gate, fixed before the runs: median paired prefill gain >= 3%, at least 4 of
5 pairs won, completion content byte-identical, and `VmSwap == 0` everywhere.

Summary of the five scored pairs:

- Median paired gain: -4.644%.
- Wins: 2 of 5 pairs favor B (+1.222% and +19.147%); the other three favor A.
- Arm medians: A (depth 3) 2977.235 ms, B (depth 4) 3115.512 ms.
- Arm-median change: B is -4.644% versus A, i.e. 138.277 ms slower on the median.

## Noise caveat

The paired gains do not form a consistent direction. They read +1.222%, -19.247%,
-9.531%, +19.147%, -4.644%: the sign alternates and the magnitude swings by roughly
+/-19% around zero, which is far larger than any plausible depth-4 effect. On the
depth-3 acceptance run the five paired gains were tightly positive
(+4.752% to +17.005%), so this swing is not a property of the workload; it is
run-to-run variance on this machine. The arm-level medians and the paired median agree
only in one thing: neither shows a gain for depth 4. Any effect of the fourth slot is
below the natural spread of these five pairs, and the point estimate is negative. The
result cannot support an acceptance claim in either direction, but it is more than
enough to reject a candidate that needed a +3% median gain and 4 of 5 wins.

## Memory impact

- Depth 3 (control A): 3 x 16 MiB = 48 MiB pinned host, the approved G3 budget.
- Depth 4 (candidate B): 4 x 16 MiB = 64 MiB pinned host.
- Delta: +16 MiB pinned (16,777,216 bytes), which puts the ring 16 MiB over the
  approved 48 MiB budget.

The extra slot is a fixed pinned allocation and allocates nothing on the device, but
it exceeds the budget that was approved for the ring-depth work. This alone is a
reason not to adopt depth 4 even before the timing result, and it is a second,
independent rejection ground.

## Verdict: REJECTED

The candidate does not clear its pre-registered gate:

- Median paired prefill gain -4.644%, below the +3% bar.
- Wins: 2 of 5 pairs, below the required 4 of 5.
- The paired spread is dominated by noise (signs alternate around +/-19%), so no
  effect is resolved, and the point estimate is negative.
- The arm medians put depth 4 at -4.644% versus depth 3.
- Pinned host rises to 64 MiB, 16 MiB over the approved 48 MiB budget.
- Content is byte-identical and `VmSwap` stays 0, so the null is clean rather than a
  correctness failure.

Recommendation: keep depth 3. The accepted PRE-1 configuration
(`GGML_CUDA_EXPERT_RING_SLOTS = 3`, EXP-2026-09-15-062) stays as is; the ring is
treated as saturated at three slots. Depth 4 is not delivered as a default and no
further depth (5 and above) is swept automatically, because a fourth slot already
shows no gain and already breaks the memory budget. This card does not build, commit
or flip any default.
