## 1 ULP mismatch — FOUND AND FIXED (2026-09-09)

- Symptom: ON differed from OFF by exactly 1 ULP (examples: 5816222 vs
  5816222.5; 21175226 vs 21175228; -379822.5 vs -379822.625).
- Cause: `hsum_float_8_e46` (ggml-cpu.c) was in the form of two `_mm_hadd_ps`,
  reduction tree `((a0+a4)+(a1+a5)) + ((a2+a6)+(a3+a7))`; stock
  `hsum_float_8` (arch/x86/quants.c:43) builds
  `((a0+a4)+(a2+a6)) + ((a1+a5)+(a3+a7))`. Float addition is non-associative —
  a divergence of exactly 1 ULP on the final hsum with a bitwise-identical
  loop body (q2/qh/signs, scales16, get_scale_shuffle_k4, sumi1/sumi2, fmadd
  per block — all matched).
- Fix: `hsum_float_8_e46` rewritten instruction-for-instruction from
  stock (extract128 / add / movehl / add_ss+movehdup).
- Confirmation: after the rebuild the ON-arm is ALL PASS (see above), the
  OFF/0-arm unchanged.
