# EXP-2026-10-31-113: GPU generation of the attention mask

Status: NOT_RUN in this session.

Motivation measured from tensor geometry: an F16 mask for a 4096-token ubatch
over 32768 KV cells is 32768*4096*2 = 256 MiB; at 131072 KV it is 1 GiB. These
are allocated in the compute buffer and filled from the host per ubatch. The CPU
fill and H2D share were not measured, and no GPU mask generator was implemented.
Preserve causal/SWA/sequence semantics and fallback if pursued.
